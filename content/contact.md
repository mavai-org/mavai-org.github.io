**Let's talk.** Software built on a language model can give a different answer to the same question, so one passing test tells you nothing. If you care whether such a service does what you say it does, a first conversation covers where your project lifecycle stands today and how its testing needs to change to give you that evidence. If a regulator is asking the same question, it is the same evidence. Use the form below — every message reaches a human.

- **General question** — about Mavai, the projects, or the methodology.
- **Project feedback** — issues, suggestions, or contributions for punit and friends.
- **Let's talk** — a baseline-and-monitor engagement for an AI or other non-deterministic service, regulated or not.
- **Consulting / advisory** — AI technical due diligence, testing strategy, or speaking enquiries.
- **Other** — anything else.
